# Recomp Plan — 3-Day Full Body + Meal Prep Nutrition

**Bodyweight:** ~96 kg · **Goal:** lose fat, gain muscle, hold weight roughly stable
**Training:** 3 lifting days/week · **Sessions:** 50–60 min

---

## Why 3 days full body beats the 5-day split

Your coach's split hit each muscle **once** per week. This one hits everything **three times** per week in fewer sessions. Same or better muscle growth, less time in the gym, and it fits how you actually want to train.

Every session includes: neck, forearms/grip, lower back, core — the things missing from the old plan.

---

## Training

Run **A → B → C**, one session per day, with at least one rest day between. Typical week: Mon / Wed / Fri, or Sat / Mon / Thu. Order matters more than the exact days.

### Rules

- **Warm-up:** 5 min incline walk, then 2 light ramp sets on the first exercise only.
- **Reps in reserve:** stop 1–2 reps short of failure on everything except the last set of isolation work. Not every set to failure.
- **Rest:** 2 min on the first three exercises, 60–90 s on the rest.
- **Progression:** when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add 2.5 kg next session. Log every set.
- **Neck:** first two weeks bodyweight only, slow, no jerking. Add load after that.

### Day A — Squat focus

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Smith squat (or goblet squat) | 3 × 8 |
| 2 | Chest-supported row | 3 × 10 |
| 3 | Incline dumbbell press | 3 × 8–10 |
| 4 | Seated leg curl | 3 × 10 |
| 5 | Lateral raise | 2 × 15 |
| 6 | Neck extension (bodyweight → plate on forehead, lying) | 2 × 15 |
| 7 | Wrist curl + reverse wrist curl (superset) | 2 × 15 each |
| 8 | Lying leg raise (flat bench, hands gripping behind head) | 3 × 12–15 |

**Progressing the lying leg raise:** knees bent → legs straight → feet on a decline bench → light ankle weights. Lower slowly and don't let your lower back arch off the bench — that's the cue that you've gone past your current range.

### Day C — Posterior chain + arms

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back extension / 45° hyperextension | 3 × 12 |
| 2 | Hammer Strength incline press | 3 × 8 |
| 3 | Single-arm lat row | 3 × 10 |
| 4 | Bulgarian split squat (or walking lunge) | 3 × 10 per leg |
| 5 | Rear delt flye | 3 × 12 |

**Core coverage across the week:** lying leg raise (Day A), cable crunch (Day B), Pallof press (Day C) — nine direct sets across flexion, hip flexion and anti-rotation. Squats, RDLs, split squats, farmer's carries and back extensions load the trunk on top of that.

**Note on incline cable press:** removed. Incline dumbbell press (Day A) and Hammer Strength incline press (Day C) cover the same angle without needing a bench at the cables.

### Cardio

**Incline treadmill walk — 30–40 min, speed 5–6 km/h, incline 8–12%.**
3–4 times a week, on non-lifting days or after lifting. Not before.

Skip the speed-10 running intervals from the old plan. At 96 kg after time off, the joint cost outweighs the calorie difference, and incline walking doesn't eat into recovery from lifting.

Daily step target: **8,000–10,000**.

## Nutrition

**Target: 2,300 kcal · 195 g protein · 70 g fat · ~220 g carbs**

That's roughly a 350–450 kcal deficit against your maintenance. Slow enough to keep building muscle, fast enough to see the fat move. Expect **0.4–0.6 kg per week** on the scale.

Three meals, not four. The four-meal thing isn't an insulin requirement — total daily protein and calories are what matter. Three meals of ~65 g protein each covers you fine.

### Sunday prep (about 75 minutes, feeds you 3–4 days)

Cook in this order so everything finishes together:

1. **1.4 kg chicken breast** — oven, 200°C, 25–30 min. Season heavily, don't add oil.
2. **1.2 kg lean beef mince (5%)** — one pan, brown it, season.
3. **1.2 kg white rice, cooked weight** — rice cooker or pot.
4. **1.5 kg potatoes** — cut into cubes, same oven as the chicken, 200°C, 35 min.
5. Bags of **frozen mixed vegetables** — microwave fresh each meal, 3 min. Don't prep these.

Box it into single portions while everything is still warm. Fridge holds 3–4 days safely; freeze anything for day 5+.

### Daily structure

**Meal 1 — breakfast (~550 kcal, 45 g protein)**

Rotate freely:

- 4 whole eggs + 3 egg whites, 2 slices whole wheat toast, 1 banana
- 150 g chicken (from prep) + 2 eggs + 1 slice toast
- 50 g oats with water, 1 scoop whey, 1 tbsp peanut butter

**Meal 2 — chicken box (~720 kcal, 70 g protein)**

- 220 g cooked chicken breast
- 200 g cooked white rice
- 150 g frozen mixed veg
- 1 tsp olive oil or hot sauce

**Meal 3 — beef box (~700 kcal, 60 g protein)**

- 200 g cooked lean beef mince
- 300 g roasted potato
- Large green salad
- 1 tsp olive oil

**Optional 4th slot if you're hungry (~200 kcal, 25 g protein)**
Whey shake in water, or 150 g chicken, or 300 g Greek yogurt when you have it. Greek yogurt is optional throughout — nothing in this plan depends on it.

### Water

**3 litres a day, minimum.** You mentioned carrying water weight — the fix is more water and consistent salt intake, not less. Cutting water makes retention worse. Creatine also pulls water *into* the muscle, which is what you want and isn't fat.

### Eating out

1. Protein first
2. One carb source only
3. No sauces or mayo
4. Stop at 80% full
5. Once every 10–14 days, no guilt

## Supplements

**Keep:**
- **Creatine monohydrate — 5 g daily**, any time, with or without food. The one supplement worth the money.
- **Vitamin D3 — 2,000–4,000 IU daily.** Worth it, especially with indoor work.

**Caffeine:**
Two black coffees is about 160–190 mg. Adding a pre-workout scoop can push you past 350 mg on training days. That's near the sensible ceiling. **Nothing after 4 pm** — sleep is doing a lot of the work in a deficit, and caffeine has a 5–6 hour half-life.

**L-carnitine — honest answer:**
Oral L-carnitine barely raises muscle carnitine levels; absorption without a large insulin spike is poor. The fat-loss evidence in trained people is weak. It won't hurt you, but don't count it toward your results, and don't let it justify a smaller deficit. Your calories and your step count are doing that job.

**Sugar:**
Cutting added sugar is a good call — but it's the calories, not the sugar itself. Fruit stays in.

## What to track

Weekly, same conditions (morning, after bathroom, before food):

- **Bodyweight** — 7-day average, not daily readings
- **Waist at the navel** — currently 104.3 cm. This is your real progress marker
- **Top set weights** on Smith squat, RDL, incline press, lat pulldown

If waist drops and lifts go up while weight barely moves, the plan is working exactly as intended. That's recomposition and the scale will lie to you about it.

**Check in at 4 weeks.** If waist hasn't moved at all, drop 150 kcal from carbs. If you've lost more than 0.8 kg/week, add 150 back.
